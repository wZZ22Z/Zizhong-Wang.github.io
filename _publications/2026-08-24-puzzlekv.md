PuzzleKV compresses the KV cache with page-wise low-rank decomposition, aligning compression granularity with paged KV-cache management in LLM serving. Attention is computed directly over the compressed factors, without reconstructing historical KV states, using fused attention kernels for mixed dense and factorized KV pages.

At 60% KV storage, PuzzleKV preserves 98.7% of Full-KV performance on LongBench and 96.2% on RULER. With INT4 factor quantization, it retains 93.4% on RULER at 18.7% storage.

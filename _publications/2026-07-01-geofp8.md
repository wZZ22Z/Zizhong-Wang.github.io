GeoFP8 transforms anisotropic gradients using approximate Fisher geometry to reduce quantization distortion during low-precision gradient communication. It is integrated into Megatron-LM with a staged asynchronous pipeline that overlaps FP8 AllReduce with gradient preparation and dequantization/mapback.

On 64 NVIDIA GH200 GPUs, GeoFP8 reduced end-to-end pretraining time for Llama-600M by 7.6% relative to FP32 gradient communication.
